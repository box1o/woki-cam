# Library sources

- Bel symbol and footprint: custom, based on the included manufacturer drawing. Three fixed units (PHY, PoE, LEDs) share one reference and one physical footprint. Pin map: symbols/magjack-pinout.csv.
- Bel STEP and FreeCAD reference: custom dimensional model generated by scripts/model.py. Not manufacturer CAD; not a production fit gauge.
- Capacitor, resistor, connector and power symbols; standard passive/header footprints; standard passive/header STEP files: installed KiCad libraries. Upstream: https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries
- KiCad library licence and design exception: https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/

All project-assigned models use paths relative to the KiCad project directory. J5 is a copper test pad and has no separate physical part/model.

MagJack pin names: A-D identify PHY channels; CT_A-CT_D are their PHY-side centre taps. VC12/36/45/78 are raw cable-side PoE taps, with no fixed polarity. Y = yellow, G = green, O = orange; A/K = LED anode/cathode. Green and orange share antiparallel pins 19/20. SHIELD is chassis, not digital ground. Internal magnetics/termination are inside the jack; the simplified symbol does not show their discrete circuitry.

# independent power audit

Reviewed 2026-11-21. Read-only circuit review of the current native Board 1 schematics, a fresh KiCad XML export (`/tmp/power-independent-now.net`), and manufacturer documents. This checks circuit intent and selected ratings; it does not establish prototype performance or PCB manufacture readiness.

The 953 Ω suggestion concerns a fault threshold, not a constant-power limiter. At its highest threshold the possible 12 V stack load is approximately 46.6 W before local loads and conversion loss; that would exceed a 51 W PD-input budget. Keep a separate normal operating load budget. Neither the old nor proposed resistor guarantees Class 6 budget compliance by itself. [TPS25947 electrical specifications and §8.2.2.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf)

## checked connections and ratings

- U510 classification resistors R568 = 31.6 Ω and R569 = 130 Ω match Class 6. R571 = 25.5 kΩ provides the detection signature. SCDIS is deliberately open for parallel status. TPH and BT pass through separate optocouplers into a NOR gate: both asserted status inputs are required to enable the stack output. Lower allocation keeps the output disabled. This agrees with the parallel status table for this requested class. [TPS23730 pin functions and tables 8-1/8-2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps23730.pdf)
- The four MagJack PoE centre taps feed eight correctly oriented Schottky diodes. The positive/negative outputs feed separate L402 windings; C401, C402 and C539 total approximately 102 nF before the hot-swap, excluding device/parasitic capacitance. Bulk C523 is on the switched return side.
- STPS3H100U is a 3 A, 100 V SMB rectifier, consistent with the selected footprint. L402 = 744272251 is rated 2.8 A, 80 V, with 35 mΩ maximum winding resistance; its nominal electrical ratings cover the intended input current. Temperature and surge performance remain layout dependent. [ST datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stps3h100.pdf), [Würth datasheet](https://www.we-online.com/components/products/datasheet/744272251.pdf)
- T502 primary pins 1/3, auxiliary pins 4/5 and parallel secondary pairs 9+10 / 6+7 match the Coilcraft drawing. Pin 4 connects to CP; pin 5 reaches the bias/feedback circuit through R545, not directly to RTN. The selected transformer has 22.2 µH ±10%, ratio 1:0.5:0.5, 12 V/3.9 A indicative output and 4.7 A saturation-current specification. That saturation figure is measured at 25°C for a 15% inductance reduction; it is not a guaranteed hot operating limit. [Coilcraft POExxQ drawing](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/71a3c6d3-d040-4587-8bbd-dd1492f81afc/poexxq.pdf)
- Q506 source pins 1/2/3 feed the R567 current shunt, drain pins 5/6/7/8 feed T502 pin 3, and gate pin 4 reaches the controller gate network. R572 = 100 Ω / C551 = 100 pF filter the CS signal. R553 = 60.4 kΩ corresponds to approximately 250 kHz.
- The PSR feedback and compensation are adapted from TI PMP22477. The winding ratio is retained but inductance differs; retained compensation values are initial prototype values. Diode-output PSR regulation depends on transformer and rectifier behavior. The advertised synchronous-output typical accuracy must not be presented as a guaranteed 12 V result. [TI reference design](https://www.ti.com/tool/PMP22477), [TPS23730 §§8.3.7.2–8.3.7.4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps23730.pdf)
- U301 VIN/EN connect to isolated 12 V, SW to L301, VOS directly to the output, FB to the divider, and AGND/PGND/exposed pad to secondary ground. R301/R302 = 312 kΩ/100 kΩ gives approximately 3.296 V. A 2.2 µH inductor and nominal 22 µF output follow the device's application structure. Exact capacitance under bias and the 1 A local load budget remain to be established. U302 operates within its 5.5 V rating; its QOD pin ties to the switched output. [TPS62160](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62160.pdf), [TPS22918](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22918.pdf)

## eFuse divider clarification

R602/R603 = 1 MΩ/100 kΩ gives approximately 13.2 V nominal OVLO. R604/R605 = 806 kΩ/100 kΩ gives approximately 10.872 V rising power-good threshold. The TPS25947 Rev C electrical table specifies OVLO leakage up to 0.1 µA and PGTH leakage up to 0.3 µA. Existing divider currents exceed twenty times those electrical-table maxima. The application example inconsistently mentions 1 µA PGTH leakage; this is not grounds to declare the current divider invalid. Scaling both dividers ten times lower while retaining their ratios is an optional noise/leakage improvement. Resistor tolerance, reference tolerance and leakage must all be included in actual threshold limits. [TPS25947 §6.5 and §§8.2.2.2/8.2.2.4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf)

## PCB requirements still open

The primary input/RTN domain must remain isolated from secondary GND, USB and stack signals. Place input bypassing, primary switching loop, current-sense Kelvin return, rectifier/output loop and buck bypassing according to manufacturer layout guidance. Keep switch nodes away from FB, PSR, clock and Ethernet routing. Establish explicit creepage/clearance rules using the actual insulation requirement and PCB process; a transformer hipot rating alone does not establish PCB isolation. The 14 mm transformer and jack cannot occupy a 3 mm interboard cavity. Physical fit, temperatures, EMC and 35 W continuous output remain unproven until the real PCB and prototype are evaluated.

No circuit edits were made by this reviewer. No fatal pin-number error was found in the inspected power blocks; that finding is bounded to the connections listed above and is not a whole-product guarantee.
